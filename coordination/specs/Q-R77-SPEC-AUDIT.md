## § Decision rationale (why-picked / why-rejected for material choices)

### Choice 1: Family C interpretation as pure-ONS bounded-z (vs full kernel-MMD)

**Picked:** Pure-ONS bet via exported `onsUpdate` primitive on the same bounded-z input as Family A.

**Why:**
- Preserves the directive's stated comparison (Family A baseline vs Family C alternative).
- Avoids synthetic `CompiledConfig` construction (would require deep type-import from `engine/types/*` that drifts across engine changes).
- Avoids ALLOWED_SET expansion (kernel-MMD baseline pool generation would require importing `generateBaselinePool` from `engine/detectors/sequential-mmd.ts`, expanding the engine dependency surface).
- Provides a methodologically-distinct comparison: A uses GRAPA/ONS-fallback dual bet + multiplicative wealth M_t; C uses pure ONS bet + additive log-space wealth log_S_t.

**Why-rejected (alternatives):**
- **Full `evaluateFamilyCBettingEProcess`:** would require synthetic CompiledConfig + baseline cells; ALLOWED_SET would need to include `engine/types/*` for type imports; runtime would need to seed RFF feature maps. Out of round scope (no engine/* modifications + no synthetic config construction per § 5 NO #16).
- **Page-CUSUM (Family A sibling):** would compare A_betting vs A_cusum (intra-family), not Family A vs Family C. Doesn't match the directive's "Family C" wording.
- **A simpler "no Family C; just Family A only":** would reduce the matrix from 504 to 252 cells but would not deliver the directive's "Family A vs Family C comparison ACs."

**Audit-trail honesty:** the directive author may have meant something other than what I interpreted. § 1.3 sub-§ records the interpretation as a LOAD-BEARING DECISION (rather than a silent choice) so the Reviewer can flag it as ESCALATE if the operator-intent diverges from the Architect's interpretation.

### Choice 2: Drift starts at window 0 (vs R72's drift_start ≥ 4)

**Picked:** drift = `magnitude * (w + 1)` from w=0.

**Why:**
- R77's deliverable is the envelope as a function of MAGNITUDE × WINDOW × α × FAMILY; R72 already varied drift_start. Adding drift_start as a 5th axis would 5× the matrix size for marginal characterization gain.
- Operationally: an operator tuning Tessera cares about "what drift magnitude can I detect with N windows?" — start-from-window-0 gives a clean envelope. drift_start mid-stream is a separate question (already covered by R72's variations).
- Comparison with R72 is preserved at the "same magnitude × same window count" level even though drift_start differs; the comparison is documented as "complementary characterization, not direct replication" in § 1.4.

**Why-rejected:**
- Matching R72's `magnitude * (w - drift_start + 1)` with drift_start as a varied parameter: 5× matrix size for marginal characterization gain.
- Matching R72 with a FIXED drift_start (e.g., drift_start=4): introduces a pre-drift period that wastes early ticks in the lower-window cells; would make low-window cells essentially never detect even at high magnitudes (the wealth doesn't accumulate enough). Distorts the envelope.

### Choice 3: 5 trials per cell (vs more)

**Picked:** 5 trials per cell, matching the directive's explicit "5 trials with different RNG seeds."

**Why:**
- Directive prescribes 5 explicitly.
- 5-trial resolution (0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, 100%) is sufficient for operator-relevant decisions (distinguishing "essentially always detects" from "essentially never detects" and the transitional band).

**Why-rejected:**
- More trials (10, 50, 100) would give finer resolution but at proportional runtime cost. The deliverable shape (single grid sweep) doesn't need adaptive resolution.
- Fewer trials (1, 2, 3) would be PRNG-noise-dominated at boundary cells (per § 0 Approach A rejected reason).

### Choice 4: 14 magnitudes (vs 15 to include 0.40)

**Picked:** 14 magnitudes [0.050, 0.075, ..., 0.375], matching the directive's "0.05 → 0.40 in 0.025 steps; 14 magnitudes."

**Why:** Directive explicitly says "14 magnitudes." Following directive literal even though 0.05 to 0.40 inclusive in 0.025 steps is 15 values (directive likely off-by-one, but the literal "14" was authoritative).

The 14 magnitudes cover up to 0.375 which is well above R72's detection-saturation point (R72 detected at magnitudes 0.3+ reliably). Excluding 0.40 doesn't lose characterization; the envelope is well-saturated by 0.30.

### Choice 5: Single-shard per trial (vs 10-shard matching R72)

**Picked:** Single-shard per trial (target shard only).

**Why:**
- Family A betting e-process is per-shard independent (no inter-shard state in `updateBettingState`).
- 10× runtime savings at no characterization loss.

**Why-rejected:** Matching R72's 10-shard would 10× runtime without changing the envelope (other shards' wealth states don't affect target-shard detection).
