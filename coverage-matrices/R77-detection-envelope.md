## Method

Each cell runs 5 deterministic trials seeded by `(SCENARIO_SEED_PREFIX ^ (cell_idx * 5 + trial_idx))`. Family A uses `engine/detectors/betting-e-process.ts:updateBettingState`; Family C uses `engine/detectors/family-c-betting-e-process.ts:onsUpdate` per R77 Family C interpretation (see Q-R77-SPEC.md § 1.3). No engine modifications; no new dependencies. Re-running `pnpm detector-envelope` produces byte-identical output.
