# 2026-07-26 — the ICC boundary is ~6–8%, not 1.5%; and per-path budgeting is not warranted

- **Artifacts:** `tools/icc-sweep.ts`; `test/icc-sweep.test.ts`.
- **Closes:** **A2-icc** (the boundary), and the per-path budgeting question opened by **N9**.
- **Depends on:** N11 — every ICC below is on the corrected axis. This sweep was deliberately gated
  on A2-host, because running it against the biased estimator would have produced a boundary with
  the same ~1.4× compression as the target it was meant to replace.

---

## 1. Result

seeds = 4, nUnits = 2016, T = 160 rounds, α_page = 10⁻³, **Ville budget = 2.016 pages/run**.
The ICC column is **measured per cell** with the corrected estimator, not assumed from the knob.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.72% | 0.132 | 0 | 0 | — |
| 2.75% | 0.168 | 0 | 0 | — |
| 4.28% | 0.212 | 0.25 | 0 | — |

**The budget is breached between ICC 6.32% and 8.36%, on both pipelines.**

## 2. The published target is conservative by 4–5×

The live figure is ICC ≲ 1.5%, which came from H16 being the only clean scenario measured. The
sweep shows the fleet is comfortably inside budget at **4.28%** (0.25 pages, 12% of budget) and
still inside at 6.32% (1.5 pages, 74%).

This matters operationally rather than academically: the design instruction is *"enrich block keys
until residual persistent heterogeneity is below the fault size you care about"*, and the cost of
that enrichment rises steeply as the target falls. Asking for ≤1.5% when ≤4% is safe is asking for
work that buys nothing.

**Recommended target: ICC ≲ 4%**, not 6%. The 6.32% cell sits at 74% of budget with 6 paged units
across 4 seeds — Poisson noise on that count is ±2.4, so "under budget" there is not a confident
statement. 4.28% has real margin and is defensible; 6.32% is the edge of what was measured.

## 3. Per-path budgeting closes NEGATIVE — and N9 predicted it

The loop path does page less below the boundary (0.75 vs 1.50 at 6.32%; 2.75 vs 4.00 at 8.36%) —
roughly the 1.5–2× one would expect. But:

1. **it crosses the budget in the same cell**, and
2. at 12.83% it is marginally *worse* (15.50 vs 14.50).

This is exactly N9's mechanism, arriving where N9 said it would. The geometric mixture buys BARRIER
height, not drift; its attenuation collapses as δ → δ₀. The units that page are by construction the
tail with δ ≳ δ₀ — precisely the regime where the mixture buys nothing. **The mixture protects the
units that were never going to page.**

So a single global ICC budget is correct, and the per-path question opened in
`research/2026-07-26-a2-mixture-margin.md` § 5.2 is answered: do not split it.

## 4. Scope, and what this is NOT

- **The two columns are PIPELINES, not accumulators.** They differ in the input transform as well as
  the accumulation rule — the accumulator path takes a conformal rank within a K+1 block, the loop
  path takes a round-standardised residual. A difference between columns cannot be attributed to the
  accumulator alone. N9 isolated the accumulation rule on matched inputs; this measures what the two
  deployed paths do to the same fleet.
- **Resolution is one grid step.** The crossing is bracketed by 6.32% and 8.36% and not localised
  within it. A finer grid would narrow it; the recommendation above does not depend on doing so.
- **Simulator, not fleet.** As ever: A2-θ-real still needs the probe pilot, and nothing here
  substitutes for it. What this sweep fixes is that the target it will be compared against is now
  both correctly scaled (N11) and empirically bracketed rather than extrapolated from one point.
- **Location heterogeneity only.** Persistent *dispersion* (A2-disp) is out of model, as everywhere
  else in the A2 line.
